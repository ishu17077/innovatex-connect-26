Step-by-Step Registration

Welcome! This guide will walk you through the process of securing your spot at InnovateX Connect ’26, happening on Sept 05 at JIS University Kolkata.

1

Visit the Official Website

Navigate to the official event portal: connect.innovatexcom.xyz

Here, you'll find all essential event details, including Speakers, Event Agenda, FAQs, and Registration Information.

Tip: Access the registration page by clicking the "GET YOUR 🎟️" button or the "Register →" button in the top navigation bar.

2

Register for the Event

Once on the registration page, you will need to create your account. Please fill in the required details carefully.

Select Your Role

Choose the appropriate tab at the top of the form. Ensure you are in the correct category before submitting.

  • Student: Requires College/University, GitHub, and LinkedIn.
  • Professional: Requires Company/Organization instead of a university.
  • Community Partner: Requires Community Name and details.
3

Verify Your Account

You have two options to verify your account during registration:

  1. OTP Verification:

    Enter your email, click "SEND OTP", and check your inbox for a 6-digit code to enter in the verification field.

  2. Google Authentication:

    Scroll to the bottom of the form and click "Continue with Google". First, authenticate with your Google account. You will then be redirected back to the form with your Name and Email automatically pre-filled, allowing you to easily complete the rest of your profile.

4

Complete & Claim Ticket

Complete your profile by selecting your Food Preference and confirming your laptop requirement.

Mandatory Requirement

InnovateX Connect'26 features hands-on workshops. Bringing your laptop is mandatory for both Students and Professionals.

Once all details are verified, click the "Register" button at the bottom. Your account is now created.

5

Access Your Dashboard

After registering, you can access your personal Dashboard anytime. This is your central hub for:

  • 🎟️ Your Ticket Status
  • 📢 Event Updates
  • 🗓️ Event Details & Agenda
  • 📋 Other Important Info

Note for Students and Working Professionals: Once your ticket request is confirmed, you will automatically steps to claim your official event pass in your registered mailbox.

Community Partner Guide

P1

Register as a Partner

Follow Step 2 above, but ensure you select the Community Partner tab under "SELECT YOUR ROLE". Provide accurate information about your community.

P2

Get Your UTM Link

Upon successful registration, navigate to your Partners Dashboard. Here, you will find a unique UTM referral link generated specifically for your community.

P3

Share Your Link

Copy your unique UTM link and share it across your community channels (WhatsApp, Discord, Slack, etc.). Both Students and Professionals can register using this link.

Action Required: Please make sure to actively share this exact link with your student and professional community. Anyone registering via this link attributes points to your community rank!
